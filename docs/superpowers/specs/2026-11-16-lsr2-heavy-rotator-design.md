# LSR-2 heavy rotator and the LSR rotor family: design spec

Status: draft for review, 2026-11-16. Authors: Anatol EA5KAO, Diego EA5JEF, Pedro Roca Molina (BricoHams).

## 1. Goal

Add a fixed-station **heavy** az/el rotator (LSR-2) to the LibreSat family, next to the portable LSR-1. It should carry large Yagi pairs or a small dish. It is our own redesign of the architecture SARCNET published for the SARCTrac Mk5. The parts are printed in PLA/PETG first, then made in aluminium by **CNC from plate** (flat parts) and **sand casting** (3D parts). One firmware, one protocol and one head software drive both rotators. The `antenaSatCommrotor` station can drive either one.

## 2. Reference and licence position

- The SARCTrac Mk5 FreeCAD files (local copy, dated 28 May 2024) come with **no licence**, only an "as-is, no warranty" note. We use them **only as a local reference**: we read the architecture and check sizes.
- No geometry, file or drawing from them is copied into this repo. All LSR-2 geometry is generated by our own scripts from our own parameters. Bought parts (NMRV030, NEMA23, 51206) use the makers' catalogue dimensions.
- `NOTICE`/`DESIGN_ANALYSIS.md` will credit SARCNET for the architecture idea and state that no files were derived from theirs.

Mk5 facts used (from the public page and our measurements of their model):

| Item | Mk5 |
|---|---|
| Drive | 2 × NMRV030 worm 80:1 + NEMA23 57BYG250H (2.8 N·m, 3 A), DM542 drivers at 48 V |
| AZ load path | 51206 thrust bearing (30/52/70 mm) in a PVC pipe, M30 washers onto the gearbox housing |
| Shafts | M14 stainless threaded rod, keyed with 5×8 roll pins |
| Structure | 10 mm aluminium saddle plates (≈280×100), galvanised angle, laser-cut covers |
| Sensing | one optical index slot per axis plus saved backlash; position saved on power loss |
| Range / speed | AZ ±360°, EL −30…150°, 12°/s |
| Controller | Pico 2W-class board running MicroPython, EasyComm II over Wi-Fi/Telnet |
| Payload | 1.8 m dish or large crossed Yagis (assembly bounding box ≈1.95 × 1.77 × 1.16 m) |

## 3. What LSR-2 changes, and why

| Mk5 weak point | LSR-2 decision |
|---|---|
| A single index slot, open loop between indexes | Absolute magnetic encoder on each **output** shaft (AS5048A/AS5600, the same closed loop as LSR-1). The index is kept only as a cross-check. |
| Thrust bearing housed in PVC pipe with silicone | Bearing housing in **cast A356**, with a machined seat, a V-ring/lip seal and a drain hole |
| Threaded rod used as shaft, roll-pin keys | Ø14 h7 stainless 1.4404 shaft with an A5×5 DIN 6885 key, matching the NMRV030 bore |
| Galvanised angle iron (corrosion, mixed metals) | Plates and brackets in 6061-T6/5083; A2/A4 fasteners with Tef-Gel or Duralac at Al–steel joints |
| MicroPython on a separate board | The **LSR-CTL RP2040 firmware** gains a step/dir output for external drivers (DM542 or TMC5160). EasyComm II and rotctld stay exactly as on LSR-1. |
| 48 V only | 24–48 V input; the 5 V and 3.3 V rails stay on the board |
| No wind or torque budget published | `calc/lsr2_sizing.py` works out the wind moment, the worm self-locking and the bearing life for each payload class, and states the safe payload limit (see §7) |

## 4. Family layout

| | LSR-1 (exists) | LSR-2 (new) |
|---|---|---|
| Use | portable, tripod | fixed mast, 40–60 mm |
| Drive | printed cycloid 25:1 + NEMA17 | NMRV030 80:1 + NEMA23 |
| Controller | LSR-CTL, internal TMC2209 | LSR-CTL, step/dir to external drivers |
| Firmware / protocol | shared: RP2040 C++, EasyComm II, closed loop | same build, flag `LSR_DRIVER=EXT_STEPDIR` |
| Head software | shared `software/lsr` (tracker, rotctld, web UI, Doppler) | same, with a rotator profile `lsr2` |
| `antenaSatCommrotor/rotor` | adds an EasyComm II/rotctld backend that drives LSR-1 or LSR-2 | |

## 5. CAD toolchain

- The CAD is **FreeCAD 1.1, scripted in Python** and run headless with `freecadcmd`. It lives in `cad/lsr2/`, with every parameter in `cad/lsr2/params.py`.
- **Why not OpenSCAD** (used for LSR-1): CNC shops and foundries need **STEP** and true drafted solids. OpenSCAD only exports meshes.
- `make lsr2` writes these outputs under `stl/lsr2/`, `cnc/lsr2/` and `cast/lsr2/`:
  - STEP for every part plus the assembly;
  - STL for the 1:1 printed prototype;
  - DXF for each CNC-cut plate, with hole and thread notes;
  - **casting pattern** STL and STEP: scaled for shrinkage, drafted, with machining allowance and core prints;
  - PDF/SVG drawings in BricoHams branding (TechDraw).

## 6. Parts and fabrication route

| Part | Prototype | Final | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saddle plates (front, left, right), platform, gearbox adapter plates, brackets | PLA/PETG 1:1 (or MDF from the DXF) | **CNC 2D**: 6061-T6 or 5083, 8–10 mm | Clamp from the DXF, threads tapped by hand; drawings note tolerances |
| AZ bearing housing and mast clamp | PETG | **Sand cast A356-T6**, then machined | Bearing seat bored to H7, sealing face machined |
| EL hub / boom clamp | PETG | **Sand cast A356**, then machined | Bore Ø14 H7 with keyway |
| Motor/gearbox covers | PETG/ASA | 2 mm aluminium sheet, bent (or kept in ASA) | |
| Encoder carriers, index disc, cable gland bosses | PETG/ASA | stay printed | Non-structural |

Casting rules, held as parameters and checked in the self-test:
- shrink scale 1.013 (A356, sand; the foundry confirms the value);
- draft 2° on outside walls, 3° on inside walls;
- machining allowance 2.5 mm on datum faces and bores;
- minimum wall 5 mm, fillets r ≥ 3 mm, no section ratio above 3:1;
- the pattern is printed in PLA at the scaled size, split along the parting plane, with locating dowels.

## 7. Engineering checks (`calc/lsr2_sizing.py`, with tests)

- Wind moment on each axis for: 2 × 2 m crossed Yagis, a 1.2 m dish and a 1.8 m dish, at 60 and 100 km/h.
- NMRV030 output torque at 80:1 against the catalogue rating, plus a check that the worm self-locks.
- 51206 static and dynamic rating against the payload weight plus the overturning moment.
- Step rate for 12°/s at 80:1 with 1/8 microstepping.
- A 1.8 m dish **probably exceeds** the NMRV030's rating in wind. The calc will say so plainly and give the safe payload limit, or the step up to NMRV040.

## 8. Verification

- **CAD self-test.** Each part is a valid solid with volume > 0, the assembly has no interference, and the NMRV030 and NEMA23 hole patterns match catalogue values. Casting patterns are larger than the finished part everywhere by the shrink plus the allowance.
- **Firmware.** Host unit tests for the `EXT_STEPDIR` driver: step timing, direction and enable.
- **Before anyone cuts metal**, measure the real NMRV030 with a caliper and update `params.py`. Then fit-test the 1:1 printed prototype.

## 9. Phases

1. Spec, sizing calc and the parameter file. **Exit:** the calc reports the safe payload.
2. CNC plates: scripts, DXF and drawings, with a printed or MDF fit test.
3. Cast parts and their patterns.
4. Firmware `EXT_STEPDIR`, an LSR-CTL header for the external drivers, and the `lsr2` profile.
5. The `antenaSatCommrotor` EasyComm backend, then the BOM, assembly docs and branding.

Heavy, mechanical generation work (the FreeCAD part scripts) is delegated to Copilot `--write` with this spec. Claude verifies every output with the self-tests and by rendering it.

## 10. Out of scope

- Commercial kits.
- Any 1.8 m dish deployment beyond the limit the calc sets.
- Elevation flip on LSR-2 (the Mk5 range of −30…150° already covers it).
- Reusing any SARCNET file.
