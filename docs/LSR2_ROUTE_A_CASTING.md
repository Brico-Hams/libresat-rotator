# BricoHams · LibreSat LSR-2: Ruta A, fundición en arena desde patrón impreso (Route A, sand casting)

<!-- palette: blue #12263f, orange #e8871e -->
Authors: Anatol EA5KAO, Diego EA5JEF, Pedro Roca Molina. Revision `params.REV` = **A0**.

> **Optional cast variant.** This route belongs to the optional cast/CNC variant (`LSR2_DESIGN=cast`, 80:1, aluminium open-box body). The default LSR-2 is the SARCTrac Mk4 replica ([LSR2_MK4](LSR2_MK4.md)), made from laser-cut 2 mm sheet, 10 mm CNC plate and cut SHS; see [BOM_LSR2](BOM_LSR2.md) and [LSR2_MATERIALS](LSR2_MATERIALS.md).

Binding brief: `reference/BRICOHAMS_LSR2_BRIEF.md`. Code: `cad/lsr2/route_a.py` (self-test `python cad/lsr2/route_a.py`, models built by `cad/lsr2/build.py` under FreeCAD). Cost: [LSR2_MATERIALS](LSR2_MATERIALS.md). Alternative: [LSR2_ROUTE_B_CNC](LSR2_ROUTE_B_CNC.md).

Every number is tagged **DATASHEET** (published, with source), **MEASURE** (needs a caliper on the real part) or **PARAMETRIC** (our choice, editable in `cad/lsr2/params.py`). Everything marked "to agree with the foundry" is a proposal: nothing here is a foundry specification. Geometry is our own, generated by our scripts (no SARCNET CAD).

## 1. Parts, models and files (Modelos)

Cast parts (`route_a.PARTS`): `body`, `lid`, `mast_bracket`, `yoke_arm` (x2, mirrored), `saddle`, `boom_clamp` (x2, mirrored). Per part `build.py` writes to `cast/lsr2/` (mm, `REV` in the report):

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `<part>_finished.step\|stl\|3mf` | FINISHED part (the solid of `body.py` / `axes.py`) |
| `<part>_preform.step\|stl\|3mf` | CASTING PREFORM = finished + machining stock on bearing faces, seats, gasket groove and datum planes; fine holes, critical threads and bearing fits left solid; temporary clamping pads where listed |
| `<part>_pattern.step\|stl\|3mf` | PRINTABLE PATTERN = preform x shrinkage, drafted, with core prints |
| `<part>_pattern_pN.stl`, `<part>_pattern.3mf` | pattern pieces that fit the print volume, with registration keys at the joints |
| `<part>_core.step` | sand core(s) incl. core prints and clearance |
| `<part>_mockup.3mf` | 1:1 dry-fit mock-up of the FINISHED part (not scaled), in print-size pieces |
| `<part>_lostpla.step\|stl\|3mf` | CONSUMABLE lost-PLA variant (section 3): separate process, separate parameters |

Build checks (assertions, `build_part`): finished inside preform; every stock pad present in the preform; scaled preform inside the pattern; pattern bounding box >= shrink x preform; core does not overlap the preform; every solid `isValid()`; every STL welded and watertight (closed 2-manifold test); every piece inside the print volume.

## 2. Casting parameters (independent)

Shrinkage, draft, stock, wall and fillet are independent keys of `params.CAST`; `params.CAST_PART` overrides them per part (lid, saddle, boom clamp: outside draft 1 deg because they are flat). Alloy candidate: **A356-T6** (Al-Si-Mg family, "to agree with the foundry"). Minimum yield 180 MPa is the value in `inputs.py` (MEASURE: foundry certificate).

| Parameter | Value | Tag | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shrink` | 1.013 | PARAMETRIC | pattern = preform x 1.013 about the part origin; foundry to confirm for the part geometry |
| `draft_out` / `draft_in` | 2.0 / 3.0 deg | PARAMETRIC | outside faces / holes and lumps; lid, saddle, clamp 1 deg outside |
| `machining` (stock) | 2.5 mm | PARAMETRIC | on bearing faces, seats, gasket groove, datum planes |
| `min_wall` | 5.0 mm | PARAMETRIC | all ribs and walls |
| `min_fillet` | 3.0 mm | PARAMETRIC | transition radius |
| `hot_spot_max` | 2.0 | PARAMETRIC | largest inscribed sphere / adjacent wall (target 1.5-2) |
| `lump_max` | 2.0 | PARAMETRIC | sand lump depth / width |
| `core_print`, `core_clear` | 30 / 0.5 mm | PARAMETRIC | core print length beyond the open face; sand clearance |
| `dowel_d`, `dowel_len`, `dowel_clear` | 8 / 10 / 0.3 mm | PARAMETRIC | pin D8, socket D8.6 |
| `pla_fill`, `series` | 0.25, 1 | PARAMETRIC | pattern infill; castings per pattern |
| `print_margin` | 6 mm | PARAMETRIC | usable print volume = `inputs.print_volume` (256 mm cube, assumed printer) - margin - dowel length |
| `draft_step` | 4 mm | PARAMETRIC | slice height of the stacked-offset draft |

Draft of the printable pattern is built as stacked 4 mm offset slices (widest at the parting plane) fused with the exact scaled half, so the pattern always contains the scaled preform. Hand check (self-test): 2 deg over 86 mm adds 3.0 mm per face.

## 3. Two separate processes (never mixed)

### 3a. Recoverable-pattern sand moulding (main route)

Printed pattern halves (PLA/PETG) are drawn from the sand and reused. Parameters: `params.CAST` + `CAST_PART` only.

| Part | Parting plane / draw | Cores (and prints) | Loose pieces / undercuts | Gating, risers, vents: **sketch only, to agree with the foundry** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| body | y = 0, draw along y (cope and drag) | 1 dry-sand core: cavity + AZ prong; print on the open top (z) | none after the y draw | sprue + choke at the rear low corner, ingates along the floor edge; risers on the AZ pillar top and the rim; vents on the core print |
| lid | top face (z = lid), plate in the drag | none | none | edge gate at a short side, 2 vents at the far corners |
| mast_bracket | y = 0, draw along y | 2: tube bore (blind, print at the bottom) and top cup + pocket (print on top) | none | ingate at the tube foot, riser on the lugs |
| yoke_arm | z = EL axis, draw along z | 1 through the Ø20 boss, print at each end | none | ingate at the foot, riser on the flange top |
| saddle | top pad face | none (the boom window is a drafted sand lump, depth/width <= `lump_max`) | none | edge gate, vents at the corners |
| boom_clamp | top pad face | none | none | edge gate |

Registration: two dowels on the parting plane at opposite corners outside the part (`flask_dowels`). The undercuts of the body (EL boss, side windows) were removed by design (bearing seats open to the cavity, 11a fix) so no loose pieces are needed.

### 3b. Consumable lost-PLA pattern (separate variant)

A hollow shell of the preform (cavity air-filled, sand poured in), burned out by the metal. Parameters: `params.CAST_LOST` only (shrink 1.013, stock 3.0 mm, shell 2.0 mm, vent Ø3 mm, no draft, no cores, no halves). Material: **low-ash PLA** casting filament. Burn-out strategy, coating, venting and sprue layout: **TO BE APPROVED BY THE FOUNDRY**. Estimated PLA = shell area x wall, reported as `lost_pla.pla_cm3`. Files: `<part>_lostpla.*`. This variant is not costed in the BOM (open decision 1).

## 4. Hot spots and section modulus (Puntos calientes)

Check per junction: largest inscribed sphere diameter / adjacent wall (`route_a.junctions`, evaluated in `self_test`). Formulas (hand-checked in the self-test: tee 5/5/r3 = 7.75 mm, elbow 5/r3 = 6.89 mm):

* tee (wall `t`, rib `s`, fillet `r`): `d = 2((s/2+r)² + (t+r)² - r²) / (2(t+2r))`
* elbow: `d = 2(√2 (t+r) - r) / (1+√2)`

Results with the 5 mm wall and r3: wall-rib tee 1.55, wall corner 1.38, lid-screw post 1.89, EL boss 1.43: pass (<= 2.0). Junctions above the limit carry a declared mitigation (the self-test fails if the mitigation text is empty):

| Part | Junction | Ratio | Mitigation (to agree with the foundry) |
|---|---|---|---|
| body | AZ pillar ring | 2.3 | chill at the pillar foot, riser on the pillar top |
| body | AZ neck + floor block | 5.4 | chill under the neck, riser through the AZ core print |
| mast_bracket | pinch lug | > 2 | chill on the lug, feed from the tube, or take route B |
| mast_bracket | shaft-pocket ring | > 2 | riser at the cup centre, or route B |

Design rule: all other ribs and walls stay at 5 mm with r3 fillets; thickness changes are tapered, never stepped.

## 5. Printing the pattern (Impresión)

All pieces fit the print volume `inputs.print_volume` (assumed 256 mm cube, MEASURE on the real printer): limit 240 mm. Larger halves are cut by bisection and keyed with a pin D8 / socket D8.6 (10 deep) at positions found inside the solid (`split_to_fit`). Formats: watertight STL, 3MF (zip with `3D/3dmodel.model`, written with `zipfile`, one object per piece).

| Part | Orientation | Supports | Joints | Dimensional check points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| body | each half parting face down | none on drafted faces; core-print stubs print as stand-alone cylinders | pin/socket on every cut | x length x shrink, y 2 x 86 half width, EL boss centre height from the parting face, cavity box after shrink |
| lid | flat, top face down | none | pin/socket | length 230, width 146, thickness with stock |
| mast_bracket | each half parting face down | none (bore and cup are cores) | pin/socket | tube OD 76, lug span, print stub diameters |
| yoke_arm | upper half plate down on the parting plane | none | pin/socket | boss centre height, flange top height above the axis plane (125), core-print stub centre |
| saddle | flat, parting face down | none (window is a drafted lump) | pin/socket | window 28 x 76, plate 8 + stock |
| boom_clamp | flat, parting face down | none | not split (fits the bed) | block 10 + stock |

Check the printed dimensions against the pattern STEP/STL with a caliper before moulding. **1:1 dry-fit mock-up kit** (`<part>_mockup.3mf`, FINISHED size, not scaled): body in print-size pieces glued on the keys, lid, mast_bracket, 2 yoke arms, saddle, 2 boom clamps, plus the real gearboxes and motors (or printed envelopes) to check screw access (shelf bolts from below, lid, gearbox faces), EL sweep and cable passage. Fit checks: lid on rim, EL bore line through both side walls, yoke arms on the EL shaft, saddle on the arms, boom in the clamps, mast bracket over a Ø60 mast.

## 6. Workshop sequence (Secuencia de taller)

1. **Approve**: foundry reviews the preform STEP, shrink, draft, stock, parting plane, cores, gating sketch and hot-spot table; freeze `REV`.
2. **Pattern**: print pieces, join on the keys, fill and smooth, check the table of section 5, seal the surface.
3. **Mould and pour**: recoverable pattern in sand with the dowels and cores; pour A356; heat treat to T6 at the foundry.
4. **Fettling**: remove gates and risers, grind flash, clean sand; keep the stock pads.
5. **Inspection**: visual for cracks, cold shuts and porosity; dye penetrant on the zones in section 7; check distortion with a straight edge on the datum faces and the lid rim; weigh against `cast_cm3` x 2.68 g/cm³.
6. **Machining** (datums first, then faces, seats, threads; set-ups as route B with the same TOL numbers): base face and datum planes, lid rim face and gasket groove, AZ seat, EL bores (one reference, `TOL.el_coax`), bearing seats, gearbox patterns, M6 threads, mast bore.
7. **Measure** against [LSR2_ROUTE_B_CNC](LSR2_ROUTE_B_CNC.md) section 5 (same numeric tolerances), then **protect** (chromate conversion or anodise with masks on fits, threads and the lid-body earth lands).

## 7. Zones that need extra inspection (Zonas críticas)

| Zone | Why | Extra inspection |
|---|---|---|
| AZ pillar ring and AZ neck | structural: carries the whole overturning load through 2 x 6206 + 51206 | dye penetrant, section check on a sacrificial casting (first article) |
| EL bosses and side walls | structural: EL bearing loads and yoke reaction | dye penetrant, bore wall check after machining |
| Lid rim, gasket groove, glands, drain | sealing (IP target, to be tested) | leak test with the gasket fitted, penetrant around the rim |
| Yoke arm boss ring and flange | structural: torque and saddle load | dye penetrant |
| Mast bracket lug and tube ring | structural: clamp preload | dye penetrant, preload test on the pinch bolt |
| Saddle plate window edges | stress raiser | visual + penetrant |

## 8. Open decisions

1. Lost-PLA variant: whether to use it at all, and burn-out/coating (foundry).
2. Real foundry: alloy and treatment, shrink, draft, minimum wall, gating (everything marked "to agree").
3. Series size (`series`): amortises the pattern PLA.
4. Real printer volume (MEASURE) and joint strength of the printed halves.
5. Hot-spot mitigations of the mast bracket: chill/riser or route B.
6. Body: the AZ neck lip z -12..-8 is solid while the shaft passes through it (11a interface to resolve); the lid-screw posts end 2 mm below the rim (probably gasket compression).
