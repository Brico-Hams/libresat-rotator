# LSR-2 technical risk review

Authors: Anatol EA5KAO, Diego EA5JEF, Pedro Roca Molina (BricoHams). REV A0, phase 11e. Scope: the Mk5-schematic architecture of [ARCHITECTURE](ARCHITECTURE.md) §7d (main body with lid, AZ low, EL high, through-shaft, U-yoke, saddle, cross boom, lift arms, counterweights, mast bracket).
Numbers come from [LSR2_SIZING](LSR2_SIZING.md) and [LSR2_INPUTS](LSR2_INPUTS.md); tolerances from [LSR2_TOLERANCES](LSR2_TOLERANCES.md). Likelihood (L) and severity (S): 1 low, 2 medium, 3 high, **before** mitigation; these are our estimates (PARAMETRIC judgement), to be revised after the first bench results ([LSR2_TEST_PLAN](LSR2_TEST_PLAN.md), test IDs in the last column).

| # | Risk | Cause | Effect | L | S | Mitigation | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Gearbox output bearing overload | NMRV030 rated 16.7 N·m (13.4 allowed with SF 1.25), peak 33.4 N·m; external loads on the hub | Worm wheel damage, play, noise | 2 | 3 | Gearboxes transmit torque only: dedicated bearings (2 x 6206 + 51206 AZ, 2 x 6004 EL); soft limits and bumpers; keep the antenna balanced (≤ 1.5 N·m residual) | S2-1, S2-3 |
| R2 | Motor stall exceeds gearbox peak | Stall torque at the output is 101 N·m against 33.4 N·m peak | Broken wheel or key on a hard stop | 2 | 3 | Soft limit margin 9.6°, elastomer bumpers (k 5e4 N/m, 5 mm), lower DM542 current; slip element if the bench shows the need (open decision) | S1-6 |
| R3 | Axis misalignment (AZ/EL perpendicularity, EL coaxiality) | Casting + machining or slab stack not bored in one setup | Bearing preload, extra backlash, pointing error | 2 | 2 | Tolerances T03, T06, T07 with justified capability; route B line-boring after bolting; pointing model removes the non-perpendicularity term | Inspection T03-T07, S3-1 |
| R4 | Pointing worse than 0.1° | Backlash (raw AZ 0.50°, EL 0.61°), stiffness, wind (0.094° / 0.090° at 25 km/h), unbalanced load | Missed satellite passes on 435 MHz | 2 | 2 | Firmware backlash compensation (residual 0.02°), index sensors, pointing model fit, balanced boom | S1-2, S1-3, S2-2, S3-1 |
| R5 | Water ingress | Lid gasket, shaft seals, glands, breather, drain, condensation | Corroded drivers and bearings, short circuit | 2 | 3 | Cord gasket (groove 2 +0.1), lip seals on the EL shaft, V-ring and labyrinth cup on AZ, M16 / M12 glands with drip loop, drain Ø4, breather; **no IP claim before the spray test** | S2-4 |
| R6 | Driver overheating | Two DM542 at 3 A in a closed box, sun on the body | Thermal shutdown, lost steps | 2 | 2 | Drivers on the inner wall pad with a thermal path through the wall and external ribs; reduce current; light-coloured finish; current ≤ 2.5 A if the bench says so | S1-8 |
| R7 | Cable wear or snagging | AZ ±360° harness wrap, EL coax coils, bend radius | Broken wire, noise, mechanical jam | 2 | 2 | Bend radius ≥ 10 × OD, LMR-240 static bend 25.4 mm, clock-spring coil at EL, cable clamps, travel test with the real harness; slip ring if the wrap fails (open decision) | S1-5 |

| R10 | Foundry defects (porosity at the bearing seats, cold shuts) | Sand casting, thin walls 5 mm | Cracked or leaking part | 2 | 3 | Machining stock 2.5 mm on seats, X-ray or dye-penetrant on the first article, route B (CNC) as fallback | Inspection, S0 |

| R13 | VHF/UHF noise from the drivers | DM542 chopping near 144 / 435 MHz | Higher noise floor, desensed receiver | 2 | 2 | Shielded motor cables with ferrites, driver chopping off the harmonics, drivers away from the coax path inside the body | S2-5 |

## Residual open decisions

Brake or not, parking rule in survival wind, 130 km/h survival (reported only), foundry and CNC machine capabilities, AZ wrap versus slip ring, Wi-Fi module fitted or not, IP target, counterweight rod height. See `LSR2_INPUTS.md` ("Open decisions") and the tolerance document.
