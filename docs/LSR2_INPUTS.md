## Open decisions

1. Real antenna masses, CGs and projected areas (weigh and measure; the table uses the old 6 kg / 0.35 m² payload).
2. Mast: diameter, wall, material and free height of the real installation; whether it can be guyed (guying removes the tilt term).
3. Maker torsional stiffness and backlash of the purchased NMRV030, and the motor torque-speed curve on the DM542 at 48 V (bench test).
4. NMRV030 input bore: caliper the real part; bushing adapter versus a supplier NEMA23 input kit with an Ø8 bore.
5. EL shaft stepping (one turned piece with a removable bearing sleeve on one side, or two half shafts keyed into the hub).
6. Foundry: alloy and treatment, shrinkage and process (recoverable-pattern sand vs consumable PLA); the consumable variant is not sized here.
7. CNC and printer: the assumed machine and bed sizes; the real ones may split the pattern or change the cavity access.
8. Brake: not needed if the bench test confirms the worm stays locked under vibrating wind; otherwise add a spring-applied brake.
9. Parking elevation and azimuth rule in survival wind (the sizing shows which elevation is better).
10. Survival wind 130 km/h parked: reported only; passing it may need a stronger gearbox or a lower antenna area.
11. Motor stall torque at the output exceeds the gearbox peak: soft limits + bumpers, or a lower DM542 current / slip element (decide from the bench test).
12. Motors forward (+x, chosen) versus rearward: "same side" in the brief is ambiguous; rearward needs yoke arms of ~284 mm (layout study row).
13. Strap supporting the forward-cantilevered motors (about 0.13 m past the body wall) if the vibration test asks for it.
14. AZ cable: helical wrap around the mast (chosen, +-360 deg) versus a slip ring.
15. Coax type: LMR-240 fits the EL clock-spring slab; LMR-400 needs ly = 0.21 m (PARAMETRIC).
16. Wi-Fi module fitted or not (external sealed bulkhead antenna) versus the LSR-CTL alternative.
17. IP target: defined but TO BE TESTED; no IP claim is made.
18. Counterweight rod height h (0 vs below the Yagi plane) and sliding versus fixed weights.
19. Printed full-size mock-up of the body to confirm screw access (shelf bolts from below, lid, gearbox faces).
20. Foundry route (11c) and CNC route (11d) derive from the same finished solid; wall/rib thickness follows params.CAST.
21. Body, lid, yoke and AZ masses from the CAD build report: if they differ from this table by > 10 % update HEAD / stat_mass / yoke_mass.
22. Mast clamp crush is checked on the Ø60.3 x 3.6 design mast; thin-wall masts down to Ø40 need a sleeve or a saddle with a larger contact.
