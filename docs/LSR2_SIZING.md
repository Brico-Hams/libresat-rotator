# LSR-2 sizing (SARCTrac Mk4 replica, design mk4)

Design **mk4**: 23HS32-4004S (2.4 N·m, 4 A) through a worm gearbox 50:1, 48 V supply, target 12 deg/s. Select with `LSR2_DESIGN=mk4|cast` (ratio override `LSR2_RATIO`).

Generated by `python calc/lsr2_sizing.py` from `cad/lsr2/inputs.py` and `cad/lsr2/params.py`. Do not edit by hand. Inputs and open decisions: [LSR2_INPUTS.md](LSR2_INPUTS.md). Each number carries its tag in the inputs table (DATASHEET / MEASURE / PARAMETRIC); results are only as good as the MEASURE rows.

**Result: all safety factors meet their target.** Total mass 26.9 kg (limit 28); moving group 17.7 kg; head 7.6 kg; worst case taken over elevation -30..150 deg and wind azimuth 0..345 deg.

**Verdict.** A single-stage 80:1 worm at a = 30 mm with the only standard module that fits (m = 0.5) carries about 3.1 N·m (bending, fatigue) and 7.8 N·m (wear), against the 13 N·m rating of the bought NMRV030: roughly 4 times short. Capacity grows with the cube of the centre distance; parity needs a ≈ 49 mm (an NMRV050-class envelope, heavier and dearer). A two-stage or coaxial pre-stage cannot fit inside the NMRV030 envelope because the NEMA23 flange axis is the worm axis. The geometry, patterns, bearings and interfaces are complete and drop-in, but with these gears the **LSR-WG30 is an option for light duty (holding at stand-still with a lower wind survival, or a different gear set), not a replacement for the NMRV030 at the loads of this head: keep the bought NMRV030.** The cost comparison is in [LSR2_MATERIALS](LSR2_MATERIALS.md).

## 16. Cables through the body glands and the harness wrap

The AZ shaft is a solid Ø14 stub (no bore): the harness is not threaded through it but wrapped 1.5 turns round the mast bracket at r 90 mm (10 x OD, rule >= 10 x OD), which absorbs the ±360° azimuth range; the coax coils on the EL axis at r 65 mm (11 x OD; LMR-240 static minimum 25.4 mm). A wireless link (Wi-Fi, the Mk5 way) removes the data cores from the harness.
